You know how fitted bedsheets never really fold right? You try to put the corners over each other, but the corners don't really match up. Bunching it up and throwing it in the drawer takes up waaay too much space and the sheets are full of wrinkles when their time comes.

What are the corners of a fitted bedsheet anyway...? The ones that would go on the top corners of the mattress? Some part of the elastic band?

So thanks, Pinterest! While randomly scrolling, I learned hot to properly fold a fitted bedsheet!
Lately, every time I manage to produce a nice square pack, I can feel my heart jump a little with joy. I hope this feeling lasts and is reproducible.

Makes me wonder what other little things I can discover that might improve my life in little ways.
I mean, think about it: how many times will I fold a bedsheet in what remains of my life? Exactly, a lot of times! A little more joy goes a long way.
